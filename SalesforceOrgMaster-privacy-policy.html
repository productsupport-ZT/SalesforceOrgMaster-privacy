Salesforce OrgMaster (the "Extension") is a Chrome extension that helps Salesforce administrators, developers and architects explore and analyze their own Salesforce org — objects, fields, permissions, metadata, Apex, dependencies, debug logs and more — using the Salesforce session they are already signed in with.

In short: the Extension works directly between your browser and your Salesforce org. Zordial has no servers that receive your Salesforce data. We do not sell data, show ads, or run analytics or tracking in the Extension. The only information that ever reaches us is feedback you choose to send.

2. Information stored on your device

The Extension uses Chrome's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local) and session storage to remember, on your device only:

  • favorites, tags, recently viewed items and usage history;
  • your SOQL / Apex run history and the last Execute Anonymous code you ran;
  • a local audit log of changes made through the Extension, ignored code-analysis issues and scan history, recent dependency-graph searches;
  • preferences such as theme, collapsed menu groups, panel opt-ins and launcher position;
  • the host name of the org you most recently connected to (to offer a quick reconnect).

This data is grouped per Salesforce org and user, stays on your computer, and is not sent to us. You can remove it at any time by clearing the Extension's data or uninstalling it in Chrome (chrome://extensions). Because it can include query text and record identifiers from your org, treat the browser profile like any other place where work data lives.

3. Actions the Extension takes in your org

The Extension reads from your org by default. It writes to your org only when you explicitly ask it to — for example when you run Execute Anonymous Apex, insert generated sandbox data, save Apex, edit metadata from the metadata editor, or toggle debug logging. Turning on debug logging creates a Debug Level named "SalesforceOrgMaster" and a Trace Flag for the user you choose, in your org. Everything runs with your own Salesforce permissions and appears in your org's normal audit and debug records.

4. Feedback you choose to send

The in-app Help & Feedback form is optional. If you submit it, the following is sent over HTTPS to Web3Forms (api.web3forms.com), a third-party form-delivery service that forwards it to Zordial's inbox:

  • the category and the message you typed;
  • your email address, only if you enter one;
  • optional diagnostics, only if the diagnostics box is ticked: Extension version, the connected org's host name, API version, the panel you were on, and your browser's user-agent string.

Do not include passwords, session IDs or customer data in feedback messages. We use feedback only to respond to you and to improve the Extension, and keep it only as long as needed for that purpose. Web3Forms processes the submission under its own terms and privacy policy.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not collect, receive or store your Salesforce data, credentials or session on any Zordial server.
  • We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.
  • We do not use analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or third-party trackers in the Extension.
  • We do not send your org's data to any AI or machine-learning service.
  • We do not load or run remotely hosted code; all code runs from the packaged Extension.
  • We do not use or transfer user data for purposes unrelated to the Extension's single purpose, or to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

The Extension's use of information received from Chrome and Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

7. Security

Traffic between the Extension, Salesforce and Web3Forms uses HTTPS. The session cookie is held in memory only, and the Extension runs under a strict Content Security Policy (script-src 'self'). Access to your org is limited to what your Salesforce user can already do. You remain responsible for securing your browser profile and Salesforce account.

8. Your choices and rights

You control the Extension: you decide which org to connect, which tools to use, and whether to send feedback. You can disconnect by closing the dashboard, clear local data or uninstall at any time. To ask what feedback-related personal data we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, contact us at the address above. Where applicable law gives you additional rights (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, or the GDPR), we will honour them.

9. Children

The Extension is a professional tool for Salesforce users and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
